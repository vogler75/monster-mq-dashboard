Virtual hierarchy

    Arrow keys move, Right/Left expand or collapse, Enter selects, Delete removes. Drag nodes to move them, or use the buttons and the Parent field. Drag a topic from the Topic Browser onto a node: dropping on a link or a mount rebinds that node; dropping on a folder adds a sub node, a leaf topic as a link and a topic with child topics as a mount (hold Shift to flip that).
